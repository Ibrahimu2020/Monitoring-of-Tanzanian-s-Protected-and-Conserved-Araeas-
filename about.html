About this system

This interactive dashboard enables you to monitor long-term and recent forest cover changes within protected landscapes, Compare forest gains and losses across Tanzania, identify areas experiencing significant forest decline or recovery, support evidence-based conservation and biodiversity planning.

What it does

The dashboard combines satellite-derived forest cover data for Protected and Conserved Areas to provide insights into how they changed over time, use a filter to choose a protected area, explore maps, charts and indicator to understand forest cover trends.

The list covers Tanzania protected and conserved areas of all types.

How to read the results

Five classes. Each index is split into five classes from red (low or bare) to dark green (very dense). Soil maps use a green scale from low to high.

Class breaks. Stretch 98% divides the 2nd to 98th percentile of the forest's own values into five equal classes, which shows variation within one forest. Standard thresholds use fixed values so different forests and dates can be compared. NBR always uses standard thresholds.

Images used. Cloudy pixels are removed and the remaining images are combined into one median image for the period you choose.
